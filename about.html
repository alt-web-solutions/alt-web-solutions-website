About Alt. Web Solutions

Helping local businesses get online, one step at a time.

Alt. Web Solutions was created after seeing how many local businesses are being left behind online, from outdated websites to no website at all, or web setups spread across too many different providers.

Based in Kerikeri and supporting businesses across New Zealand, Alt exists to make web services simpler, more approachable, and easier to manage for local business owners.
