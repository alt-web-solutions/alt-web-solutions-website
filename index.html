07 / FAQ

Before we start.

A few practical answers for business owners considering a new website, refresh, or managed care plan.

Do I need hosting or a domain already?

No. If you already have them, Alt. can work with your current setup where suitable. If not, hosting and domain options can be discussed before launch.

Can you redesign my current website?

Yes. Alt. can help with website refreshes, redesign concepts, or rebuilding an outdated site into something cleaner, clearer, and easier for customers to use.

Do you manage the website after launch?

Yes. Managed Web Service is available for clients who want small updates, website checks, hosting and domain support, and practical help after launch.

What do I need to provide?

Usually your logo, business details, service information, contact details, images, social links, and access to any relevant website, hosting, domain, or platform accounts.

Is copywriting included?

Basic wording and layout support may be included depending on the project scope. Larger copywriting, rewriting, or content planning can be quoted separately.
