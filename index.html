08 / FAQ

Before we start.

A few practical answers for business owners considering a new website, refresh, e-commerce store, or managed setup.

Do you only build websites, or can you manage the full setup?

Alt can help with the full setup, including website design, hosting, domain management, business email setup, SSL, launch support, and ongoing website care. The goal is to keep the key parts of your online presence managed in one place.

Can Alt help if I already have a website?

Yes. Alt can review, refresh, or rebuild existing websites that feel outdated, hard to use, or no longer reflect the business properly. Existing website projects are scoped based on the current site, content, platform, and any migration needs.

Can you help if my website, domain, and email are with different providers?

Yes. Alt can help review your current setup and, where practical, bring your website, domain, business email, hosting, and support into a simpler managed setup.

Do you support businesses outside Kerikeri?

Yes. Alt is based in Kerikeri and supports businesses across New Zealand remotely.

Do you offer e-commerce websites?

Yes. Alt can help with online store setup for businesses that want to sell products online. E-commerce projects are quoted based on store size, product requirements, payment setup, shipping needs, and support requirements.

Do you offer website subscriptions?

For eligible projects, Alt may offer website subscription options. This allows businesses to get online without a large upfront website build cost, with the website, hosting, domain, business email, SSL, and ongoing care included in one predictable monthly payment over a minimum term.
