Timing

When a refresh makes sense.

A website refresh or rebuild is a good option when your business already has a website, but it feels outdated, hard to manage, difficult to use on mobile, or no longer matches the quality of your work.

  • The website looks dated
  • It is hard to update
  • It does not work well on mobile
  • Contact details or services are unclear
  • The business has changed since the site was built
  • The website is spread across old platforms or providers
  • You want to bring hosting, domain, email, and support into one managed place

Improvements

What can be improved.

Alt can review the current website and recommend practical improvements based on the condition, size, and goals of the project.

  • Layout and visual structure
  • Mobile experience
  • Service pages
  • Contact options
  • Calls to action
  • Image presentation
  • Page speed basics
  • Basic SEO structure
  • Hosting/domain/email setup
  • Ongoing support after launch
